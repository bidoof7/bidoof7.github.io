# Today I'd like to write my first blog

Hello, this is my first blog. Today, I decided to write a blog for myself to preserve my memories, events that have happened in my life and what I learn along the way. The topic that I am writing about today is also the one that is bothering me but I don't know who to talk to to relieve stress so I decided to talk to myself here.

Right now, I have a lot of thoughts about IT in general and the programming industry specifically during the AI era. I can't claim to know everything since I'm just a junior in this field. In my view, everything is changing rapidly thanks to AI, which is helping to bridge the gap between technology and humans so that anyone can create a website or personal application independently. It has also helped me grow a lot because I can reference and look up knowledge from it. I tried vibe coding, and it is really much better at coding speed than I am; it allows me to complete my work more neatly and increases my coding productivity. After a while, I gradually forgot the feeling of typing out each line of code as I imagined it. Then I tried learning Go and decided to study from official documentation and attempt to build some things related to CLI and API. Even though the process takes a lot of time, and my code might not be clean, and it may have bugs, it feels more familiar to me as I began approaching programming from the lines 'USES CRT; VAR' in Pascal. I might be starting to go against the trend; I'm beginning to explore lower-level programming languages, becoming more curious about hardware, how they communicate, and their issues. Regardless of the era, the value of programming remains, the core knowledge and mindset of a programmer still exist, but the time for stumbling and falling is still there, so let's keep moving forward. Good luck.

_"Stay hungry, stay foolish" - Steve Jobs (2005)._
